How this works

Draw the process on the Board the way you would in Arcweave. The Swimlane beside it is the same map, arranged for you.

On the board

  • Add a card: double-click empty space, or use + Step and + Decision.
  • Write in it: double-click the card. Enter finishes, Shift+Enter adds a line, Esc cancels.
  • Connect: drag from a gold dot on a card to another card. Drop on empty space to make the next card. The arrow leaves from the dot you dragged.
  • Shape an arrow: click it. Drag an end dot round its card to change the side it leaves or arrives on (or onto another card). Drag the middle dot to bend it; double-click that dot to straighten. While you drag, the side it will attach to lights up in gold.
  • Owners: set them all up with the Owners button. Double-click a card's coloured header to pick its owner.
  • Label an arrow: double-click it, or select it and type in the panel.
  • One at a time: click a card or an arrow and it is the only thing that responds; the rest fade a little. Click empty space to let go of it.
  • Select several: drag a box around them, or Shift-click. Drag one to move them all.
  • Both views: with Both chosen, the button beside it switches between side by side and stacked (board above, swimlane below).
  • Move around: hold Space and drag (or drag with the right mouse button). Scroll to zoom.
  • Delete removes, Ctrl+D duplicates, Ctrl+Z undoes, arrow keys nudge.

How a card becomes a swimlane box

  • The owner is the lane. Pick who does the step in the panel on the right.
  • A question is a decision. End the text with "?" or choose Decision; it becomes a diamond.
  • Start labels with Yes or No. Yes arrows are green and No arrows red. An arrow that goes back to an earlier step is a loop, in yellow.
  • The ideal path is the shortest way through over plain and Yes arrows. It gets the blue glow. You can force it on or off per arrow.
  • Phases: select the cards of a phase (drag a box round them), then choose "Phase for all" in the panel or right-click one of them. Or right-click the first card of a phase and choose "Start a new phase here". Name phases in the Map panel (click empty space to see it).
  • Order in time follows the arrows. Where two cards could go either way, the one further left on the board comes first.

From Arcweave

  • Export the project as JSON. If it arrives as a zip, unzip it and open project_settings.json with Open (or drop it on the board).
  • Card title = owner. Card text = the step. A card with "?" or a branch = a decision. "[P2]" at the start of a card starts phase 2.
  • Open a newer export of the same board later and this map is updated in place: your phases, pins, arrow sides, colours and box sizes are kept.

Getting it out

  • Print phases: one letter-landscape page per phase. Choose "Save as PDF" in the print window.
  • Images: a PNG of each phase and of the whole process, to copy or download.
  • Download PDF: the same phase pages as a PDF file, with no print window.
  • Save file downloads the map to your computer as a .map.json file, and Open file loads one back (or drop the file on the board). Copy / paste JSON shows the same thing as text, to send to someone.
  • Nothing is uploaded. Freestyle runs in your browser. The map on screen is also remembered in this browser between visits, but the file you save is the copy to keep.

Owners

Everyone who owns a step. Each owner is a lane in the swimlane, in this order. Set them all up first, then just pick one on each card (double-click a card's coloured header).
